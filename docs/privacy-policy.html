VotRite Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

VotRite ("we", "our", or "the app") is operated by Voterite Inc (d/b/a VotRite). This Privacy Policy explains what information the VotRite mobile application and website (votritemobil.com) collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. Protecting the privacy and integrity of your vote is our highest priority.

Information We Collect

VotRite only collects information necessary to verify your eligibility to vote and to record your ballot securely:

  • Voter verification information — such as a PIN/access code, and, where required by the election administrator, identifying details used solely to confirm you are an eligible voter for a specific ballot.
  • Ballot selections — the choices you make on your ballot, including candidates, write-in entries, and proposition responses.
  • Language and accessibility preferences — such as your chosen ballot language and reading speed, so we can present your ballot correctly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your eligibility to access and cast a specific ballot.
  • To record and submit your vote accurately and securely.
  • To present the ballot in your chosen language and accessibility settings.
  • To maintain the integrity of the election (for example, preventing a ballot from being cast more than once).

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use your ballot selections for any purpose other than recording your vote.
  • We do not display advertising in the app.

How Your Information Is Protected

Voter verification is protected by a secure PIN, and your ballot selections are transmitted over secured connections to the election system. Access to election data is restricted to authorized election administrators. Sensitive identifying information used for verification is handled using protective measures designed to keep it confidential.

Data Retention

Voting records are retained by the responsible election authority in accordance with applicable election laws and records-retention requirements. VotRite does not retain your information beyond what is necessary to conduct and certify the election it was provided for.

Children's Privacy

VotRite is intended for use by eligible voters and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Choices

Voting through VotRite is voluntary. If you have questions about the information collected for a particular election, please contact the election administrator responsible for that ballot, or contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
