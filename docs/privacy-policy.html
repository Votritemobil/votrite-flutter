VotRite Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

The VotRite mobile application ("VotRite", "the app") is published by VoteRite, Inc., d/b/a VotRite. This policy explains exactly what the app sends, what it does not collect, and how that information is used. It is written to be checked against the app's actual behaviour rather than to cover us with generalities. Protecting the secrecy and the integrity of your vote is the entire purpose of this system.

Information the App Sends to the Election System

When you vote, the app transmits only the following:

  • Your PIN / access code — the single-use code issued to you by your election administrator. It confirms that you are entitled to cast the ballot you selected, and it ensures that the same ballot cannot be cast twice.
  • Your ballot selections — the candidate, party, or proposition choices you make, and the text of any write-in candidate you enter.

Information the App Does Not Collect

  • The app does not ask for or transmit your name, address, email address, or telephone number.
  • The app does not collect your location, and requests no location permission of any kind.
  • The app does not collect a device identifier or an advertising identifier, and contains no advertising software.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting software.
  • The app requests exactly one Android permission: internet access, which it needs in order to reach the election server.

Information That Stays on Your Device

Your chosen ballot language and your audio and reading-speed settings are saved on your own device, so that the app presents the ballot the way you left it. These settings are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

How the Information Is Used

  • To confirm that the PIN presented is valid for the ballot selected, and that it has not already been used.
  • To record and count your vote.
  • For no other purpose.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not use your ballot selections for any purpose other than counting your vote in the election for which it was cast.
  • We do not display advertising in the app.

How the Information Is Protected

All communication between the app and the election system is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to election data is restricted to authorized election administrators.

Data Retention and Deletion

Ballots and election records are retained by the election authority responsible for the election, in accordance with the election and records-retention laws that apply to it.

Please note that a cast ballot cannot be withdrawn, changed, or deleted on request. That is not a shortcoming of the software; it is a requirement of running a trustworthy election. Once a ballot is cast it forms part of the count. It is not linked to your name in the app in any event. Questions about election records should be directed to the election authority that conducted the election.

Children's Privacy

VotRite is intended for use by eligible voters and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
