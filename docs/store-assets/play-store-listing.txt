----------------------------------------------------------------
VotRite makes voting accessible to everyone.

Built for real elections and designed from the ground up for voters who are blind, have low vision, or simply want a clearer, guided voting experience, VotRite lets you cast your ballot with confidence — by touch, by sight, or entirely by ear.

ACCESSIBILITY FIRST
• Full spoken guidance — every ballot, race, candidate, and proposition is read aloud, clearly and completely.
• Vision mode with simple home-row key controls and tactile feedback, so you can navigate without needing to see the screen.
• Adjustable reading speed and clear, high-contrast visuals for low-vision voters.
• Every on-screen instruction is also spoken, so nothing is missed.

VOTE IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• Ballots and guidance available in multiple languages, including Spanish, Chinese, Russian, Polish, and more.
• The full ballot — candidate names, propositions, and instructions — is presented in your chosen language.

SIMPLE, GUIDED VOTING
• Step-by-step flow: choose your ballot, pick your language, confirm your identity, and vote.
• Vote for candidates, add write-in candidates, and answer propositions — with clear confirmation at every step.
• Review your full ballot before you cast it, and edit any choice.
• Overvote protection prevents you from accidentally selecting too many candidates.

SECURE & PRIVATE
• Your identity is verified with a secure PIN.
• Your selections are private and protected.
• Clear review-and-confirm screen before your vote is final.

VotRite is part of a US accessibility voting initiative committed to making sure every eligible voter — regardless of ability or language — can vote independently and privately.

Vote your way. Hear it. See it. Vote it.
